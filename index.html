Core Knowledge (Assessment Task 1)

1. Reasons for water testing

  • Monitor water quality over time & detect change
  • Establish baseline data
  • Detect nutrients, pollution, contamination
  • Meet environmental permit requirements
  • Investigate fish kills / adverse events
  • Compliance with drinking water standards

3. Guidelines & standards

Australian Drinking Water Guidelines, ANZECC Guidelines, esdat.net, Analytical Services Tasmania (AST) holding times.

4. Equipment identification

Turbidity tube – measures NTU (cloudiness). Fill, look down, read scale. Clean & dry after use.
Refractometer – measures salinity (ppt). Calibrate with distilled water, place 2-3 drops on prism, read boundary line.

5. Sample preservation

a) Chill immediately to slow biological/chemical change.
b) Freeze within 24h if not analysed same day; hold up to 28 days (nutrients).

6. Chain of Custody (AST)

a) Records who possessed samples from collection to lab arrival.
b) Ensures integrity & legal defensibility.

7. Labelling & packaging

a) Label: date, site, test, time collected.
b) Group each site’s samples in labelled bag; keep cool & dark in insulated esky.

8. Parameter definitions & procedure

Temp (°C) – thermometer/sonde.
pH (0-14) – meter/strips.
EC (µS/cm, mS/m) – conductivity sensor.
DO (mg/L, % sat) – DO probe.
Turbidity (NTU) – turbidity tube, Secchi disk, sonde.

9. AST sample containers

Total nutrients: 250mL plastic, cool/freeze, 28 days.
Dissolved nutrients: 50mL plastic, filter 0.45µm, freeze, 28 days (nitrite 2 days).
Algal ID: 375/500mL plastic, Lugols 1% v/v, 6 months.

10. Field directional changes & hazards

Hot still sunny day, shallow algal bloom: Temp ↑, DO ↑ (day) ↓ (night), pH ↑ (day) ↓ (night). Hazard: UV, heat, man overboard. Controls: sunscreen, life jacket.

Windy cloudy day, no bloom: Temp/DO/pH stable, turbidity ↑. Hazard: rough water, cold. Controls: warm/wind gear, life jacket, consider postponing.

11. Lugols calculation

500 mL × 1% = 5 mL Lugols preservative.

• Slips/trips – clear paths, non-slip footwear
• Exposure – sunscreen, hats, hydration

Completion & Dispatch (Task 5)

Sample packaging & transport

  • Group samples per site in labelled zip-lock bags.
  • Place in insulated esky with ice bricks (keep < 4°C).
  • Complete Chain of Custody form.
  • Deliver to AST or courier with ice.

AST Submission Form (excerpt)

Chain of Custody: Relinquished by (sampler) → Received by lab.
Hazards: If algal toxins, mark “Cyanobacteria” and use PPE.
Project: “AHCLPW313 Field Sampling”, PO #12345.

Post-fieldwork

  • Clean equipment: rinse sonde, turbidity tube, refractometer with distilled water; dry and store.
  • Report faulty equipment to supervisor.
  • Enter data into water quality database (validated, legible).
  • Notify supervisor of any urgent results.
Traceability: All samples accompanied by AST submission form with full Chain of Custody. Samples held for 28 days (frozen) or 6 months (Lugols-preserved algae).

Equipment & Reference Standards

Equipment operation & maintenance

  • Multi-sonde: Rinse with distilled water, store with moisture cap, calibrate before each use.
  • Turbidity tube: Clean with soft brush, rinse, dry; store upright.
  • Refractometer: Calibrate with distilled water (0 ppt), wipe prism with lens tissue.
  • Secchi disk: Lower slowly, record depth of disappearance; clean after use.

Water quality standards (Australia)

  • ANZECC 2000 – freshwater & marine guidelines
  • Australian Drinking Water Guidelines – health-based limits
  • AST Holding Times – AST hold times
  • esdat.net – environmental standards database

Test kits & chemical safety

Nitrate/Phosphate kits: check expiry, use PPE (gloves, goggles), dispose of waste according to SDS. For dissolved nutrients, filter through 0.45µm syringe filter into 50mL tube.

Forms, Chain of Custody & Links

AST Submission Form – Key fields

  • Safety: Hazards (e.g., algal toxins, hydrocarbons) – signed by submitter.
  • Customer details: Name, invoice contact, ABN.
  • Chain of Custody: Relinquished by → Received by (date/time).
  • Sample ID & analyses: Tick boxes for pH, EC, nutrients, metals, algal ID, etc.
  • Preservation & hold: Indicate if samples require freezing or acidification.
