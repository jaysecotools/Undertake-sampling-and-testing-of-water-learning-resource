# AHCLPW313 – Water Sampling & Testing
## Integrated Lesson Plan with App-Based Learning

**Course:** Certificate III in Conservation and Ecosystem Management  
**Delivery Mode:** Blended (Classroom, Field-Based Learning & App-Assisted)  
**Total Nominal Hours:** 40  
**Sessions:** 10 (4 hours each)

---

## Session Plan

### Session 1: Introduction to Water Sampling & Testing
- **Learning Outcomes:**
  - Understand the importance of water quality testing
  - Identify key legislative and workplace requirements
  - Recognise different water sampling techniques

- **Activities:**
  - **App Activity:** Navigate to **Tab 1** – Why test water? Use the interactive cards and Australian context section to facilitate group discussion.
  - **Presentation & Video:** Use content from **Tab 1** as a presentation guide. Watch recommended videos from **Tab 8** (Resources & Glossary).
  - **Self-Directed Learning:** Explore **Tab 1** and **Tab 2** (Key parameters) to research local water bodies and their uses.

- **Teacher Prompts:**
  - *"What are some key factors affecting water quality in our region? Use the 'Australia's unique challenges' section in **Tab 1**."*
  - *"Why do different industries have different water quality standards? Check the 'Compliance & regulations' card in **Tab 1**."*

---

### Session 2: Planning for Water Sampling
- **Learning Outcomes:**
  - Develop a water sampling plan
  - Identify safe work procedures and risk assessments

- **Activities:**
  - **App Activity:** Groups complete a case study using the "Sampling Plan" template from **Tab 8** (Downloadable resources).
  - **Workshop:** Hazard identification using **Tab 4** (Planning & safety). Complete the "Risk Assessment" template from **Tab 8**.
  - **Self-Directed Learning:** Read **Tab 4** (Planning & safety) to prepare for the field trip.

- **Teacher Prompts:**
  - *"What risks might be present when sampling in different environments? Use the 'Risk assessment essentials' in **Tab 4**."*
  - *"How can you ensure consistency in sampling techniques? Review the 'Quality assurance' guidelines in **Tab 3**."*

---

### Session 3: Field Techniques for Water Sampling
- **Learning Outcomes:**
  - Demonstrate correct water sampling techniques
  - Follow chain of custody procedures

- **Activities:**
  - **Pre-Field Briefing:** Review **Tab 4** (Planning & safety) and **Tab 5** (Samples & dispatch) for checklists.
  - **App Activity (In Field):** Students access **Tab 3** (Equipment & methods) and **Tab 5** (Samples & dispatch) on mobile devices for real-time reference.
  - **Self-Directed Learning:** Document field observations using the "Field Data Sheet" template from **Tab 8**.

- **Teacher Prompts:**
  - *"Why is it important to label and preserve samples correctly? Check the 'Preservation & storage' section in **Tab 5**."*
  - *"What factors can influence sample contamination? Review the 'Best practice' list in **Tab 3**."*

---

### Session 4: On-Site Water Testing Methods
- **Learning Outcomes:**
  - Conduct on-site testing for pH, temperature, and dissolved oxygen

- **Activities:**
  - **Practical Session:** Use handheld meters guided by **Tab 3** (Equipment & methods). Use **Tab 9** (Simulator) to predict how conditions affect readings.
  - **App Activity:** Interpret real-time results using the "Normal Ranges" table in **Tab 6** (Interpreting results).
  - **Self-Directed Learning:** Research common water pollutants using **Tab 2** (Key parameters).

- **Teacher Prompts:**
  - *"How do environmental conditions impact on-site test results? Use the simulator in **Tab 9** to see the effect of temperature on DO."*
  - *"What limitations do field tests have compared to lab tests? Compare information in **Tab 3** (Field) and **Tab 6** (Interpretation)."*

---

### Session 5: Laboratory Testing & Data Interpretation
- **Learning Outcomes:**
  - Understand laboratory analysis techniques
  - Interpret water quality data

- **Activities:**
  - **Guest Speaker/Video:** Use resources from **Tab 8** (Resources & glossary) to find lab tours or AST links.
  - **App Activity:** Data analysis workshop using **Tab 6** (Interpreting results) and the "Trigger Levels" table in **Tab 8**.
  - **Self-Directed Learning:** Write a reflection on lab processes using **Tab 5** (Samples & dispatch) – Chain of Custody section.

- **Teacher Prompts:**
  - *"What are the advantages of lab testing over field testing? Compare **Tab 3** and **Tab 6**."*
  - *"How can data trends inform environmental management decisions? Use the 'Case study' in **Tab 6** as an example."*

---

### Session 6: Assessing & Reporting Water Quality
- **Learning Outcomes:**
  - Compare test results to water quality guidelines
  - Document findings in a report

- **Activities:**
  - **App Activity:** Group presentations using data from field work. Justify conclusions using guidelines in **Tab 1** and **Tab 6**.
  - **Report Writing:** Use **Tab 7** (Test your knowledge) and **Tab 8** (Resources) as reference.
  - **Self-Directed Learning:** Review a government water quality report using the app to understand terminology.

- **Teacher Prompts:**
  - *"What challenges arise when interpreting water quality data? Refer to 'Common issues & what they mean' and 'Diurnal and seasonal variation' in **Tab 6**."*
  - *"How can reporting influence environmental decision-making? Review the 'When to report urgently' checklist in **Tab 6**."*

---

### Session 7: Water Quality Management Strategies
- **Learning Outcomes:**
  - Identify water quality management strategies
  - Understand remediation methods

- **Activities:**
  - **App Activity:** Use the "Scenarios" in **Tab 7** (Test your knowledge) to discuss pollution events and brainstorm responses.
  - **Self-Directed Learning:** Research catchment management best practices using **Tab 8** (Resources) and the case study in **Tab 6**.

- **Teacher Prompts:**
  - *"What are some common causes of water contamination? Review **Tab 2** (Key parameters) to understand what each pollutant indicates."*
  - *"How can land management practices improve water quality? Consider the findings from the 'Healthy' vs. 'Eutrophic' scenarios in **Tab 9** (Simulator)."*

---

### Session 8: Advanced Water Testing Techniques
- **Learning Outcomes:**
  - Conduct advanced testing methods (turbidity, salinity, EC, BOD)

- **Activities:**
  - **Hands-On Practical:** Use advanced sensors referencing **Tab 3** (Equipment & methods).
  - **App Activity:** Data comparison exercise using **Tab 9** (Simulator) to change parameters and replicate field vs. lab comparisons.
  - **Self-Directed Learning:** Research macroinvertebrates in water quality assessment using resources in **Tab 8**.

- **Teacher Prompts:**
  - *"How do different pollutants impact water ecosystems? Use the **Tab 9** Simulator to see how high nutrients and temperature affect DO."*
  - *"What role does biodiversity play in water health monitoring? Relate this back to the 'Protect human & ecosystem health' section in **Tab 1**."*

---

### Session 9: Final Field Assessment
- **Learning Outcomes:**
  - Demonstrate proficiency in field sampling and testing
  - Apply risk management strategies

- **Activities:**
  - **App Activity (In Field):** Independent field assessment using **Tab 5** (Samples & dispatch) and **Tab 4** (Planning & safety) as quick-reference guides.
  - **Peer Review:** Students give feedback using best-practice guides in the app.
  - **Self-Directed Learning:** Prepare summary report using "Field Data Sheet" and "Sampling Plan" templates from **Tab 8**.

- **Teacher Prompts:**
  - *"What challenges did you encounter in the field? Could you use the app's troubleshooting tips to resolve them?"*
  - *"How would you improve your sampling approach next time?"*

---

### Session 10: Review & Assessment Submission
- **Learning Outcomes:**
  - Finalise and submit reports
  - Reflect on learning outcomes

- **Activities:**
  - **App Activity:** Complete the **Tab 7** (Test your knowledge) quiz as a final self-assessment.
  - **Peer Presentations:** Students present reports using the app to show data and decision-making.
  - **Final Assessment Submission:** Submit reports built using the app's templates.
  - **Group Discussion:** Reflect on learning using the "Your progress" bars and key takeaways from each tab.

- **Teacher Prompts:**
  - *"How has your understanding of water sampling changed? Reflect on your journey through the app's 9 tabs."*
  - *"What are some key takeaways from this unit? Use the 'Ready for assessment!' checklist in **Tab 8** to guide your final thoughts."*

## Assessment Overview

| Assessment Task | Weighting |
|-----------------|-----------|
| Practical Demonstration (Field & Lab Sampling) | 40% |
| Report Writing (Data Interpretation & Comparison) | 30% |
| Knowledge Test (Legislation & Methods) | 20% |
| Self-Reflection Assignment | 10% |

### How the App Supports Assessment

- **Practical Demonstration:** The app serves as the **reference guide** during fieldwork, ensuring students follow correct procedures and checklists (Tabs 3, 4, 5).
- **Report Writing:** The app provides the **templates and data sheets** that students can use to structure their reports (Tab 8).
- **Knowledge Test:** **Tab 7** (Quiz) and the glossary in **Tab 8** are the ideal study tools for this assessment.
- **Self-Reflection:** The "Your progress" bars in the app, combined with the final review checklist in **Tab 8**, provide a clear structure for students to assess their own learning journey.
